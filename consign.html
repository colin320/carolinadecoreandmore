Give it a second home

Consign With Us

Have quality furniture, decor, art or collectibles you no longer need? We'll find them a new home and pay you when they sell.

How it works

Four simple steps

  1. Reach out

    Send us a few photos and a short description using the form below, or call the shop.

  2. Drop off

    If it's a good fit, we'll set up a time for you to bring your items in.

  3. We sell it

    We price your pieces, display them in the shop and share them with our shoppers.

  4. Get paid

    When your item sells, you get your share of the sale.
